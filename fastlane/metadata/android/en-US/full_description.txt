Card Games gathers well-loved patience games in one app, with its own card design. Every game keeps its own progress, missions and achievements.

• Klondike: The classic patience, every deal solvable.
• Spider: Two decks, build runs, clear the table!
• Free Cells: Every card in view: no luck, just thinking!
• Pyramid: Make thirteen, topple the pyramid!
• Three Peaks: Keep the chain going, clear all three peaks!
• Golf: Fewest strokes, clean course!
• Yukon: No stock, everything is on the table!
• Scorpion: Same-suit runs, patience and planning!
• Forty Thieves: Two decks, eight foundations, lots of thinking!
• Canfield: The starting rank changes every deal!
• Accordion: Squeeze fifty-two cards into a few piles!
• Clock: Let every card find its hour!

Highlights
• 100,000 levels per game; every deal is checked by a solver, so every one can be won.
• Hints, undo and one-tap auto finish.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions and achievements.
• Four-color cards for color blindness, 33 languages.
• Plays offline. Works on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting, games of chance or 18+ content.

"Remove ads" and "Premium" purchases apply to every game in the app.
